Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

This privacy policy covers the Android apps Toilette Paris (com.pointallen.toilet.paris), Toilette Londres (com.pointallen.toilet.london) and Toilette New York (com.pointallen.toilet.newyork). They are published by Haythem Souissi. Contact: pointallen@gmail.com.

These apps are independent. They do not represent the City of Paris, the Mairie de Paris, the Mayor of London, the Greater London Authority, any London borough, the City of New York, or any other government entity. They do not provide government services.

What the apps do

Each app shows public toilets on a map, highlights the nearest one, and lets you read and publish a rating, a comment, a photo and a status (open, closed or out of order).

Information the apps use

  • Location. If you allow it, the app reads your device location on the phone to centre the map and to show distance to nearby toilets. That location is not uploaded to our server and is not stored by us.
  • Map tiles. The map is drawn with tiles from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org). Requesting a map sends the area you are viewing to that service. OpenFreeMap’s own terms apply to that request.
  • Reviews you publish. If you rate a toilet, write a comment, report a status, or add a photo, that content is sent to our server and shown to other people who use the same app. A photo is optional and is taken or chosen by you.
  • Device identifier. With each review we send an identifier generated on the device. It is used only so that one device can keep a single editable review per toilet. It is not your name and it is not used to advertise to you.
  • Advertising identifier. The Android apps show Google AdMob ads (an ad when the app opens, and a banner). Google may use the Android advertising ID, IP address and device data to deliver and measure ads, including personalised ads where your device settings allow it. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone settings. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts inside the app. If you email us, we use that message only to answer you or to handle a deletion request.

Why we use this information

  • To show toilets near you and the distance to them.
  • To display reviews, photos and open, closed or out-of-order reports to later users.
  • To keep one review per device for each toilet.
  • To show ads that support the free apps.

Who we share it with

  • Reviews, comments, photos and status reports are visible to other users of the same app.
  • Ad requests are handled by Google AdMob.
  • Map tiles are requested from OpenFreeMap.
  • We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

Location used to centre the map is not kept on our server. Reviews, comments, photos, status reports and the device identifier attached to a review stay on our server until you ask us to delete them, or until we remove them because they are abusive or no longer needed.

To ask for deletion of a review or a photo, email pointallen@gmail.com and describe the toilet and the review. We will delete the stored review and its photo.

Official sources of toilet information

The apps show information published by other organisations. Those organisations do not operate these apps.

  • Paris — official “Sanisettes” dataset, City of Paris: opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/sanisettesparis
  • London — toilet locations come from OpenStreetMap, not from a government file: openstreetmap.org/copyright. The Greater London Authority separately publishes information about public toilets: data.london.gov.uk/dataset/public-toilets-2l8k5
  • New York — official “Public Restrooms” dataset, City of New York: data.cityofnewyork.us and catalog.data.gov/dataset/public-restrooms

Hours and availability can change. Check the source above.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will replace this page and the date at the top will change.

Politique de confidentialité

Dernière mise à jour : 22 novembre 2026.

Cette page couvre les applications Android Toilette Paris, Toilette Londres et Toilette New York, éditées par Haythem Souissi (pointallen@gmail.com).

Ces applications sont indépendantes. Elles ne représentent pas la Ville de Paris, la Mairie de Paris, le maire de Londres, la Greater London Authority, un borough, la Ville de New York, ni aucune autre administration. Elles ne fournissent pas de service public.

Données utilisées

  • Position. Si vous l’autorisez, l’application lit la position de l’appareil pour centrer la carte et afficher la distance. Cette position n’est pas envoyée à notre serveur et nous ne la conservons pas.
  • Tuiles de carte. La carte est affichée avec OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org). Afficher une zone envoie cette zone à ce service.
  • Avis. Une note, un commentaire, un signalement (ouvert, fermé, en panne) ou une photo est envoyé à notre serveur et visible par les autres utilisateurs de la même application. La photo est facultative.
  • Identifiant d’appareil. Chaque avis est accompagné d’un identifiant créé sur l’appareil, uniquement pour limiter un appareil à un avis modifiable par toilette.
  • Publicité. Les applications Android affichent des annonces Google AdMob (une à l’ouverture et une bannière). Google peut utiliser l’identifiant publicitaire. Vous pouvez le limiter dans les réglages du téléphone. Politique de Google : policies.google.com/privacy.

Aucun compte n’est demandé. L’application ne collecte pas votre nom, votre adresse e-mail, votre numéro de téléphone ni vos contacts. Nous ne vendons pas ces informations.

Conservation et suppression

Les avis, photos, signalements et l’identifiant lié à un avis restent sur notre serveur jusqu’à votre demande de suppression, ou jusqu’à ce que nous les retirions. Pour faire supprimer un avis ou une photo, écrivez à pointallen@gmail.com.

  • New York — jeu officiel « Public Restrooms » : data.cityofnewyork.us et catalog.data.gov/dataset/public-restrooms
